## Why cashback can be rejected

A rejected transaction does not automatically mean the platform is dishonest. Cashback is conditional on the merchant approving the referred sale. Common causes include:

- Returning or cancelling the order
- Using an unapproved coupon
- Buying excluded products or gift cards
- Purchasing through a marketplace seller that is not eligible
- Switching devices during checkout
- Opening another affiliate or cashback link
- Using a store from the wrong country
- Failing to meet a new-customer requirement
- Exceeding a promotion’s cashback cap
- Using a payment method excluded by the merchant
- Installing a browser extension or ad blocker that interferes with tracking

ShopBack’s terms also state that cashback is an internal record used to calculate qualifying transactions, and the final amount may depend on merchant validation and the applicable promotion.

If a purchase does not appear, check whether the merchant’s terms support a missing-cashback claim. Keep the order number, purchase date, itemized receipt, and total paid. Those details are more useful than a screenshot of the product page alone.
